# Code of Conduct

Zen expects direct, respectful, and technical collaboration.

## Expected behavior

- Keep discussion focused on the work and its impact.
- Disagree with technical claims using evidence and specific examples.
- Assume good intent while still being clear about risks and mistakes.
- Make room for maintainers to keep the product scope narrow.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or behavior.
- Repeatedly derailing technical discussion after maintainers have made a scope decision.
- Publishing private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and may restrict participation when behavior interferes with productive project work.
